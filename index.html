    Settings & backup

    Reminders

    While Battle Rhythm is open, reminders pop up on screen (and as iPad notifications if you turn them on). iPad web apps can't wake up while closed, so for alarms that always fire, even with the app closed and no signal, add your tasks to the iPad Calendar app.

    Backup & restore

    Your tasks are saved only on this device. Export a backup now and then (save it to Files or email it to yourself) so you can restore if the app is deleted or you move to a new iPad.

    Import tasks

    Bring in tasks from the battle rhythm you already have in Word, Excel, PDF or plain text. You pick which tasks to add and how often each repeats.

    Categories

    Create a category here, then pick it from the drop-down when you add or edit a task. Edit a name to rename it everywhere.

    Your categories

    Print or save a PDF

    A list of every task in a category: how often it repeats, the dates it's due, when it was last done, and a blank column for initials.

    On iPad, Download PDF opens the share sheet: pick Save to Files, Mail, or Print.

    Import from a document

    Pick your current battle rhythm and the app lists the tasks it finds. You choose which ones to bring in, how often each repeats, when it starts and its category. The file is read on this device only. Nothing is uploaded.

    Works with Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PDF, CSV and text files. Tables, bulleted lists and weekly calendar grids are all understood. On iPad, files in iCloud Drive, OneDrive, Teams or email attachments saved to Files can be picked.
