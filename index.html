    Settings

    Reminders

    While Battle Rhythm is open, reminders pop up on screen (and as iPad notifications if you turn them on below). iPad web apps cannot wake themselves up when closed, so for alerts that always fire, even with the app closed and no signal, add your tasks to the iPad Calendar app.

    Backup & restore

    Your tasks are saved only on this device. Export a backup now and then (save it to Files or email it to yourself) so you can restore if the app is deleted or you move to a new iPad.
